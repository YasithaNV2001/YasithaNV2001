## 🚀 &nbsp;Featured Work

### 1. FreelanceShield — Tax Compliance SaaS &nbsp; <sub>[`freelanceshield.in`](https://freelanceshield.in)</sub>

A production SaaS for Indian freelancers, built and shipped solo.

- **Four-regime tax engine** — computes old, new, actual-expense and 44ADA presumptive
  regimes in parallel against FY 2025-26 slabs, returning a ranked comparison from a
  single income input.
- **GST logic** — auto-selects CGST/SGST or IGST from client state, with LUT handling
  for zero-rated exports.
- **TDS reconciliation** — ingests Form 26AS CSV exports and flags mismatches by
  deductor and quarter.
- **Also**: PDF invoice generation, multi-sheet Excel export, e-signature workflows,
  tiered subscription quotas with server-side enforcement.

`Next.js` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `React` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Supabase (PostgreSQL + RLS)` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Google OAuth` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `REST APIs`

### 2. Uncertainty Estimation for AV Perception &nbsp; <sub>[`av_uncertainty`](https://github.com/YasithaNV2001/av_uncertainty)</sub>

An experimental comparison of uncertainty estimation methods for a traffic-sign
perception model (GTSRB), built in PyTorch.

| Method | Accuracy | ECE | OOD AUROC | OOD AUPR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Softmax baseline | 0.9022 | 0.0506 | 0.8953 | 0.9318 |
| MC Dropout (T=30) | 0.9025 | **0.0120** | 0.9218 | 0.9476 |
| Deep Ensemble (M=5) | **0.9318** | 0.0179 | **0.9481** | **0.9662** |

- MC Dropout bought **calibration** rather than accuracy — a 4x ECE reduction at
  effectively unchanged accuracy.
- Deep ensembles won on both accuracy and out-of-distribution detection (SVHN).
- Includes accuracy–rejection curves, coverage at a 95% target-accuracy threshold, and
  a 15-condition corruption sweep (3 types × 5 severities) for aleatoric uncertainty.

`PyTorch` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `torchvision` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `CNNs` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Deep Ensembles` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `MC Dropout` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `OOD detection`
